# MISWAY — Drift 3D Art Direction Bible

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the visual, emotional and spatial art direction for the MISWAY Drift 3D map.

The Drift 3D experience is not a racing game, not a realistic open world, and not a decorative music catalogue.

It is a playable inner map.

The user moves through eras, tracks, memories, emotional states and musical territories. The vehicle is not a car. It is a small signal module, a listening capsule, a fragile navigator moving through the creator’s internal geography.

The map must feel like:

* a musical territory;
* a mental cartography;
* an autobiographical symbolic world;
* a journey through eras;
* a sparse immersive site;
* a lambda-world: ordinary, personal, strange, universal.

The goal is not to copy Bruno Simon’s visual style. Bruno Simon is only a reference for:

* fullscreen immersion;
* vehicle-led exploration;
* clarity of interaction;
* world-as-interface;
* playful spatial navigation.

MISWAY must remain:

* darker;
* more musical;
* more adult;
* more mental;
* more ambiguous;
* less cartoon;
* less gamey;
* less “portfolio toy”;
* more like an inner sonic atlas.

---

## 2. Core myth

MISWAY is the story of an ordinary man who becomes a signal.

The lambda symbol is central because it carries two opposite meanings:

1. lambda as “ordinary”, “common”, “one among others”;
2. lambda as a sign, a gate, an opening, a strange personal emblem.

MISWAY must embrace that paradox.

The world is not about becoming superhuman.
It is about remaining human, flawed, fragile, competent, damaged, sensitive, useful, and still moving.

The player does not enter a heroic fantasy world.
The player enters a map made from work, doubt, family, cities, music, exhaustion, repair, night, light, and persistence.

The creator is not represented as a character.
He is represented through territories.

## 3. Global visual grammar

### 3.1 General mood

The global set design should be:

* nocturnal;
* sparse;
* symbolic;
* tactile;
* slightly dirty;
* sometimes beautiful;
* sometimes uncomfortable;
* always readable.

Avoid decorative excess.

Each zone must be clear enough to be navigated, but abstract enough to remain poetic.

The world should feel like a landscape of emotional evidence.

Premium does not mean muted. Drift can be vivid, saturated and expressive as long as the forms stay controlled and legible.

### 3.2 Allowed visual language

Use simple 3D primitives:

* boxes;
* planes;
* cylinders;
* cones;
* rings/torus if already available through Three primitives;
* thin masts;
* low platforms;
* ramps;
* vertical slabs;
* circular marks;
* repeated modules;
* simple horizon markers.

Use material and composition more than complex geometry.

Prefer:

* low height structures;
* clean silhouettes;
* symbolic landmarks;
* spatial rhythm;
* contrast between empty and dense areas.

Primitive-only means building visible scenic structures from simple geometry, not making tiny, invisible or purely decorative props.

### 3.3 Forbidden visual language

Do not create:

* cartoon island world;
* realistic landscape;
* realistic city;
* realistic cars;
* imported 3D models;
* character avatars;
* 3D text labels;
* emojis;
* neon cyberpunk overload;
* particle systems;
* excessive bloom;
* busy decorations;
* collectible/game UI;
* random clutter;
* abstract fog or color variation without readable landmarks;
* realistic terrain simulation;
* physics gimmicks;
* theme-park props.

The map must remain premium and restrained.

### 3.4 Readable set design

Drift 3D must not rely only on fog, hue shifts or abstract floor marks. Those can support the scene, but they are not enough by themselves. Each era needs visible set pieces, a major landmark and a clear silhouette language. If the user only perceives atmosphere, the lot has failed.

### 3.5 Drift visual rupture principle

The rest of the MISWAY site can stay dark, white, restrained and minimal. Drift is the colorful rupture of the site. It is allowed to be saturated, vivid and memorable. Color is not decoration only; it is part of the musical and mental identity of the route.

### 3.6 Visual pass acceptance criteria

A Drift 3D visual lot passes only if:

* each era is identifiable within 3 seconds from the fixed oblique camera;
* if the user sees only color/fog variation, the lot fails;
* each era has at least one major landmark and readable silhouette language;
* the result shows places, not only abstract color zones;
* vehicle, nodes and corridors remain readable;
* production/static preview smoothness is the gate, not dev-mode behavior.

## 4. Entry — Lambda Cave

### 4.1 Narrative role

Before the map, there is darkness.

The user begins in a dark cave-like threshold, close to the mood of the website’s first-page hero visual.

At the end of this dark passage, there is an opening shaped like lambda.

This opening is:

* a gate;
* a wound;
* a symbol;
* an exit;
* a birth canal;
* a passage from darkness to the sonic map.

The Entry Node is not a normal track zone.
It is the threshold before the actual map.

### 4.2 Mood

* very dark;
* mineral;
* quiet;
* almost sacred;
* slightly oppressive;
* magnetic.

### 4.3 Visual elements

Use:

* dark floor;
* dark enclosing forms;
* low fog;
* one distant lambda-shaped negative opening;
* faint silver/blue light leaking through;
* minimal signal pulse near the spawn point.

Do not use:

* bright welcome screen;
* decorative portal;
* fantasy cave;
* literal 3D text;
* cartoon opening.

### 4.4 Palette

* near black;
* graphite;
* cold silver;
* deep blue;
* very faint gold only as a distant hint.

---

## 5. Map structure

The full map is organized around four eras:

1. Birth Yard
2. Older Shadows
3. Vegetative Field
4. New Signal

These are not merely chronological folders.
They are emotional territories.

Each era should have a distinct spatial identity, palette, material mood and density.

The map should read as a journey:

Entry Cave
→ dense dirty birth-city
→ adventure / altitude / Africa / risk
→ flat comfortable routine field
→ inner symbolic new world of shadow, gold, silver and color.

# ERA 1 — Birth Yard

## 6. Birth Yard overview

### 6.1 Meaning

Birth Yard is not a peaceful beginning.

It is a city.
It is noise.
It is crowd pressure.
It is violence, dirt, social compression, work injunction, nightlife, vice, movement and mechanical behavior.

This era is about being born into the machine.

### 6.2 Mood

* urban;
* dense;
* dirty;
* violent;
* compressed;
* loud even when silent;
* full of repeated human-machine patterns.

### 6.3 Spatial density

Birth Yard should be the densest era.

Use:

* vertical structures;
* narrow corridors;
* repeated blocks;
* crowd-like modules;
* small alleys;
* low orange/blue night light;
* dirty metallic materials.

The player should feel surrounded, not free.

### 6.4 Palette

Primary:

* charcoal black;
* dirty concrete grey;
* sodium orange;
* rust red;
* deep night blue.

Accent:

* pale canal reflection;
* jazz gold;
* subway white.

### 6.5 Materials

* matte concrete;
* dull metal;
* wet dark surfaces;
* dirty glass suggestion through flat planes;
* low emissive strips used sparingly.

### 6.6 Era landmarks

The era landmark can be a dense urban cluster:

* towers;
* rails;
* crowd modules;
* alley openings;
* canal strips;
* underground entrances.

No realistic city.
Use city as symbolic compression.

# ERA 3 — Vegetative Field

## 10. Vegetative Field overview

### 10.1 Meaning

Vegetative Field is the hum of work and daily life.

It is not ugly.
It can be comfortable, almost beautiful.
But it is flat.

It is routine, artificial happiness, nihilism, non-said things, empty spaces, and the feeling that everything must start again.

### 10.2 Mood

* morne;
* flat;
* comfortable;
* repetitive;
* numb;
* beautiful but muted;
* administratively calm;
* emotionally underfed.

### 10.3 Spatial density

Use medium-low density.

Compared to Birth Yard:

* fewer vertical forms;
* more horizontal spread;
* flatter surfaces;
* more repeated low blocks;
* more empty space.

The player should feel like the world is functioning but not alive enough.

### 10.4 Palette

* off-white;
* dusty yellow.

Accent:

* faded color spots;
* chalk white;
* slow-time blue.

### 10.5 Materials

* smooth matte surfaces;

* office carpet abstraction;
* chalky marks;
* low contrast.

### 10.6 Era landmark

A flat comfort plateau:

* low grids;
* office blocks;
* pale floor;
* repeated modules;
* long empty distances.

# ERA 4 — New Signal

It is color, shadow, light, gold, silver, fear, acceptance, thought overload, work at night, paternity, duty, dissociation, fragile strength and renewed sense.

It is deeply personal, but also lambda: ordinary, common, shared.

It must not feel like pure triumph.
It must feel like a world that has survived.

Some zones are empty and freezing.

## 14. Gameplay atmosphere notes

These are not mandatory for the next visual lot. They are future design notes.

### 14.1 Possible local gameplay modifiers

Only implement later if explicitly requested.

* TIME: subtle slowdown.
* OVERTHINK: route visually harder to read.
* TELATELABA: mild maze / mirrored confusion.
* ASITIS: colder, stiller, more exposed.
* HOLD THE LIGHT: safe halo / refuge feeling.
* RELATIVE: upward/rebound path.
* LE MONDE S’ENDORT: lights progressively lower as the player approaches.

Do not add gameplay modifiers in a pure visual atmosphere lot unless the lot explicitly asks for it.

### 14.2 Navigation principle

MISWAY should not feel like a car simulator.

The preferred movement model is:

* 2D-intuitive navigation;
* fixed camera orientation;
* 3D spatial presence;
* vehicle visually points toward movement direction;
* camera follows by translation, not yaw rotation.

## 15. Implementation hierarchy

When translating this bible into code, prioritize:

1. readability;
2. navigation clarity;
3. emotional identity;
4. performance;
5. restraint.

Do not prioritize:

* visual density;
* technical tricks;
* decorative complexity;
* literal illustration;
* cinematic effects that break interaction.

## 18. Quality bar

A successful Drift 3D set-design pass should make the user feel:

* “I know where I am emotionally.”
* “Each era has a different climate.”
* “The map tells a story without text.”
* “The lambda world is both personal and ordinary.”
* “This is not a music player with decoration. This is the music as territory.”

A failed pass would feel like:

* generic low-poly map;
* random decorative zones;
* too literal;
* too gamey;
* too bright;
* too busy;
* too similar everywhere;
* not connected to the creator’s actual story.

## 19. Final direction

MISWAY Drift 3D should become:

A dark lambda cave opening into a playable sonic map.

A dirty city of birth.
An adventurous old world of height, risk and roots.
A flat vegetative field of routine and artificial comfort.
A new inner signal world of shadow, color, gold, silver, storms, mirrors, work, light, children, duty and repaired matter.

The vehicle is small.
The world is not huge, but it feels meaningful.
The player does not collect tracks.
The player crosses states.

MISWAY is the map of someone ordinary who kept turning experience into signal.
