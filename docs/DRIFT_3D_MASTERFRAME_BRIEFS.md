## 1. Entry — the frame

**The shot:** the vehicle sits motionless at the exact midpoint of the exit corridor, seconds before the eye-adaptation burn. **Owner-provided source (`OWNER_DIRECT`, GOV-40 correction):** dark mineral cave; visual continuity with the MISWAY hero image; luminous λ-shaped exit; transition from confinement into the colored world — this determines the frame's geography and light below, not only its mood.

- **Geography:** a single unbranching mineral corridor, walls close enough to read individual rock texture at this distance; no side passages, no ambiguity about which way is forward. The corridor's far end — where the λ-shaped exit sits — must maintain **strict compositional continuity** with the MISWAY site's own hero image: the same silhouette, proportion and framing relationship (`OWNER_DIRECT`, clarified by owner decision this session). **This is not a literal pixel or exact-color reproduction requirement** — the composition must be recognizably the same shape and relationship, not an identical rendering, leaving a future Identity Contract free to choose the exact camera angle within that constraint.
- **Architecture:** none — the only built element is the control apparatus itself: a low mechanical relay housing set into the rock wall, a stamp arm mid-stroke, a small unmanned screen showing a rejected/accepted glyph cycling on its own.
- **Humans:** none. The vehicle is alone; the verdict ("NON CONFORME") has already been rendered onto a surface near the relay, unattended.
- **Activity:** water drips from one point on the ceiling, striking the floor on an irregular but real rhythm — the only movement in frame besides the vehicle.
- **Material:** raw, PBR-textured stone (`rock_boulder_dry`-class material, already in the repo) — no polish, no artificial finish anywhere.
- **Light:** near-total darkness. The only light sources are the relay's small indicator and, far ahead at the very end of the corridor, a λ-shaped glow — sculpted into the rock silhouette itself, never a floating icon. This glow's warmth register should echo the MISWAY hero image's own light quality **in spirit** — compositional/tonal kinship, not a literal color-value match (owner-clarified this session) — the site's identity and the world's threshold read as kin, not as a copy of the same file.
- **Weather:** none (interior).
- **Vehicle relationship:** the vehicle occupies the full width of the corridor's drivable path — there is no room to feel small in open space here; the frame is about the walls, not the sky.
- **Density:** `SPARSE` on every axis — this frame's power comes from how little is in it.
- **Anomaly:** the verdict itself, already visible in this frame as a plain fact (a rejected stamp on the wall) — not yet the transformation into admission, which happens only once the vehicle moves toward the λ.

## 2. Birth Yard — the frame

**The shot:** dusk, the vehicle mid-canal on the approach to EUX GAINENT's glass storefront — now, reworked per owner decision, a visibly working, crowded quay rather than a quiet approach: a lifting bridge mid-cycle holding a small queue of foot/bike traffic, Foolfoule's tower silhouettes alive with lit windows and at least one tower still visibly worked late. **Owner-provided source (`OWNER_DIRECT`, GOV-40 correction, reworked per owner decision — "too sparse and calm"):** city, swarming life, violence, dirt, traffic, work and collective pressure — this must now be **visibly present in the frame itself**, not only implied by texture and mood.

- **Geography:** a canal at water level, polder-flat, port city rising on one side.
- **Architecture:** the glass-fronted gym façade catches the last warm light; behind and above it, dense vertical commercial towers (Foolfoule's register) recede into blue dusk haze, several visibly under active use — a crane still working late on one rooftop, a delivery hoist mid-lift on another; a lifting bridge sits open mid-cycle, holding a small visible queue of bikes and one delivery van at its near edge.
- **Humans:** three athlete silhouettes visible through the gym glass, mid-cadence, backlit by the fluorescent strip; **additionally (reworked)** a loose cluster of 4–6 pedestrian/cyclist silhouettes held at the lifting bridge's queue — waiting, one checking a phone-shaped silhouette, one visibly impatient posture — ordinary collective pressure made visible, not merely implied. Foolfoule's own dedicated crowd density still belongs to its own frame, but this frame no longer reads as empty of people.
- **Activity:** a towel conveyor visibly moving inside the gym; one lit office window several floors up, on too late for the hour; at the quay edge, a delivery vehicle idles too close to a cyclist's path, a held near-miss beat (`OWNER_DIRECT`: "traffic," "friction"). **One deadpan absurdity (added per owner decision):** a parking-enforcement figure methodically ticketing a bicycle that is correctly, properly chained to the base of a "no parking" sign — a functional-inversion/impossible-bureaucracy gag (Global Art Direction §3's own allowed humor families), delivered without a punchline beat, visible only if the frame is examined, never announced.
- **Material:** the gym's PBR glass/concrete/brushed-metal palette in the foreground; brick and concrete tower materials receding into atmospheric fog. The quay itself carries real wear — oil-stained concrete, a torn poster edge, standing water, a cracked curb — the frame's "dirt" register, applied to the working city's surfaces, never to the gym façade itself, which stays the era's one deliberately clean object (the Identity Contract's own contrast).
- **Light:** Birth Yard's dusk register — warm horizontal key light low in frame, cooling rapidly with distance; the gym's cold fluorescent interior reads as a deliberate temperature contrast against the warm exterior dusk.
- **Weather:** clear, still air — canal surface near-mirror, though not a spotless one.
- **Vehicle relationship:** low, close to the water, the vehicle's own reflection breaking the canal surface.
- **Density:** `VERY_HIGH` on every axis in this frame now (Global Art Direction §4's own era target band) — foreground activity (the bridge queue), background massing (towers), human presence (the held crowd plus the gym athletes) and behavioral loops (conveyor, crane, hoist) all visibly active at once. This is a deliberate change from the prior draft, which under-populated its own foreground against the era's `VERY_HIGH` doctrine.
- **Anomaly:** present but dormant in this frame — the gym reads as an ordinary storefront at first glance; nothing in this shot alone reveals the machines-train-the-people inversion.
- **Systemic violence, made perceptible without gore or caricature (owner requirement):** none of the above is graphic — the "violence" register lives entirely in friction (the near-miss), pressure (the held queue) and absurd institutional pettiness (the ticketed bicycle) — never a depicted injury, threat, or caricatured aggressor.
- **Owner-review note, `ACCEPT` (final, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40`):** systemic pressure and friction are staged directly in this frame rather than left to texture alone. This clears the generic prohibited-interpretations list (no gore, no caricatured aggressor, no shock-value staging). Crowd, traffic, work, dirt, pressure and deadpan systemic absurdity are all retained as owner-confirmed.
- **On the deadpan-absurdity gag (owner clarification, final):** the ticketed-bicycle beat — and any similar "congratulatory machine" or institutional-irony idea a future track might introduce — is **one example of an allowed behavioral family** (Global Art Direction §3: functional inversion / impossible bureaucracy / empty performance), **never a mandatory gag** Birth Yard's other four tracks must repeat. Each track may invent its own instance from the same allowed families rather than reusing this exact one.

## 3. Older Shadows — the frame

**The shot:** the vehicle on an open mountain traverse, mid-morning, a cairn trail visible ahead quietly missing one stone that was there a moment ago — now, reworked per owner decision, visibly shared with other travelers and carrying memory across the whole trail, not one object. **Owner-provided source (`OWNER_DIRECT`, GOV-40 correction, reworked per owner decision — "too empty"):** fun, African travel, extreme sports, mountain, **physical freedom becoming memory** — both halves of this material (present-tense activity, retrospective memory) must now be visibly, distributedly present, not carried by a single detail or left to subtext.

- **Geography:** an open plateau traverse between forest line and exposed ridge — Rise/Blossoming register, "breathing" open circulation, the era's contrast to Birth Yard's density.
- **Architecture:** a single distant refuge structure, small in frame, smoke or no smoke `[TO BE DECIDED IN RISE'S OWN IDENTITY CONTRACT]`; otherwise no built structure — the mountain itself is the architecture.
- **Humans:** **added per owner decision** — a distant paraglider mid-descent along the ridge line (Blossoming's own register, legible only as a small colored canopy shape at this distance, no face or detail, per the Global Art Direction's own human-presence doctrine's silhouette-only rule), and, closer, one small hiking figure crossing the trail on foot well ahead of the vehicle's own path — both read as ordinary travel/extreme-sport activity, not staged for the camera.
- **Activity:** a raptor circling at altitude; taut prayer-flag-style fabric moving in real wind; the cairn trail ahead, one stone in the act of settling into a new position on its own; the paraglider and hiker, above.
- **Material:** exposed granite/rock-boulder PBR material, patches of snow at the tree line, a worn dirt path — **and, per final owner confirmation, distributed further**: one piece of worn equipment left beside the trail (a faded strap or buckle, weathered past use), a second, older path visible eroding into the mountainside a short distance from the current one (an erased route, no longer traveled but not yet gone), and a scattering of faint footprint/track impressions in a patch of softer ground, already half filled-in — traces of previous passages, not just the cairns.
- **Light:** clear, high-altitude, cooler and more contrasty than Birth Yard's dusk — hard shadows, a single coherent sun source, per the Realism Bible's non-negotiable one-source-per-segment rule.
- **Weather:** wind, visible in the flags and the fabric — no precipitation in this specific frame.
- **Vehicle relationship:** the vehicle is small in a wide frame — the emotional register here is "movement and altitude make you feel alive," which reads through scale contrast, not proximity.
- **Density:** `MEDIUM`, open — background (mountain chains) reads `HIGH` in massing; foreground now carries two legible human/activity elements rather than none, per the owner's rework.
- **Anomaly:** the self-assembling/self-erasing cairn trail, caught mid-adjustment — visible but not yet explained, Level 1 of a Living-World-Bible-style anomaly hierarchy.
- **Owner-review note, `ACCEPT` (final, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40`):** visible travel/extreme-sport activity (paraglider, hiker) and the four-register distributed memory device above are both retained as owner-confirmed.
- **Memory device, distributed across four registers (owner requirement, final — "do not make one aged stone carry the era alone"):** (1) the *entire* cairn trail visibly mixes generations of markers — roughly a third of the stones read as older, weathered, partially lichen-covered, standing in loose clusters rather than singly; (2) one piece of worn equipment left beside the trail; (3) a second, older, eroding path visible nearby — a route no longer traveled; (4) faint, half-filled footprint traces in softer ground. The self-assembling mechanic continues to add fresh stones only at the trail's leading edge. "Freedom becoming memory" is now legible across the whole frame — cairns, equipment, an erased route, and traces of passage together — rather than any single found object carrying it alone.

## 4. Vegetative Field — the frame

**The shot:** a residential lotissement street at flat midday light, two identical houses, one sprinkler running, one "satisfaction" screen visible through a window, nothing wrong yet — now, reworked per owner decision, one credible human routine visible in frame, not only autonomous machines. **Owner-provided source (`OWNER_DIRECT`, GOV-40 correction, reworked per owner decision):** work, daily routine, comfortable flatness, artificial happiness, functioning without fully living — the human figure below is this rework's answer to "functioning without fully living," not the machines alone.

- **Geography:** repetitive suburban housing grid, laid out with the identical-lotissement logic the Vegetative Field Era Contract specifies.
- **Architecture:** two near-identical houses in frame, same massing, same render/asphalt materials, differentiated only by trivial details (a garage door color, a hedge height) — the sameness is the point.
- **Humans:** **added per owner decision** — one resident, mid-routine at their own driveway (coiling a hose, or wheeling a bin to the curb — a small, wholly unremarkable task), moving with the same unhurried, looping cadence as the robot vacuum and sprinkler beside them. **One slight desynchronization, added per final owner confirmation:** the resident fumbles the routine for a single beat — the hose slips half a coil before they catch it, or they pause a moment too long before wheeling the bin the last step — a small, real human error the machines beside them never make. This is what keeps them human, not a robot: the machines repeat exactly; the resident repeats *almost* exactly, and that gap is the frame's own quiet tell.
- **Activity:** a robot vacuum crossing a driveway on its own path; a sprinkler running on an automatic cycle; through one window, a small screen displaying a green "satisfaction" indicator to no one; the resident's own routine, above.
- **Material:** clean rendered stucco, manicured lawn (Vegetation Kit turf/hedge archetypes), fresh asphalt — everything reads maintained, nothing reads worn, which is itself the unsettling note the era's own stated goal ("the funniest cold and the most unsettling in hindsight") depends on.
- **Light:** flat, slightly overcast midday — deliberately unremarkable, no dramatic key light; Vegetative Field's register is calm, not moody.
- **Weather:** still, dry, unremarkable.
- **Vehicle relationship:** the vehicle passes through at ordinary residential speed — this is not a dramatic traverse, it is a drive down a normal street, which is exactly what makes the eventual chain-reaction anomaly land.
- **Density:** `MEDIUM`, repetitive — now including one human figure at the same repetitive cadence as the era's machines, per the Global Art Direction's own human-presence doctrine (§9): human presence here is deliberately as unremarkable as the machinery around it, never a relief from it.
- **Anomaly:** entirely dormant in this frame — this is the "everything works so well" baseline the era's own identity requires before any contamination is visible; the frame contains no visible break, by design.
- **Owner-review note, `ACCEPT` (final, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40`):** the resident is never named, never shown in close detail, and performs no dialogue or explicit gesture toward the camera — the human-presence doctrine's silhouette/timing/comparative-behavior rule applies here exactly as it does to every other track's crowd. **The one-beat desynchronization above is the load-bearing detail, not decoration:** it is precisely what keeps this resident a person and not a literal robot (per the owner's explicit requirement) — the machines' exact repetition is what's unsettling; a human's *near*-repetition, with its one small error, is what confirms they are still a person functioning inside that unsettling system, not a fourth machine.

## 5. New Signal — the frame

**The shot:** dawn, a bend in the coastal road partway along New Signal's own route — reworked per owner decision away from Étééaooété's own literal terminus, toward an overlook point earlier along the same road, where the vehicle is stopped because the view demands it, not because it has arrived. **Owner-provided source (`OWNER_DIRECT`, GOV-40 correction, reworked per owner decision — "the beach alone is too specific to the final track"):** return of color, an inner world, shadow and light, **gold and silver**, reconstruction without erasure, lambda becoming an ordinary world form — reworked so the frame expresses **convergence** across the era's own sub-arcs (ocean, signal/light, inner world, distant city, storm, reflection), rather than reading as one specific track's own frame.

- **Geography:** a coastal overlook — the road curves along a headland; the final beach (Étééaooété's own frame, unchanged and still that track's own) is visible far below and ahead, small, not yet reached; inland and behind, the land the road has already crossed.
- **Architecture:** none close — but far along the shoreline's curve, barely legible at this distance, the last few lights of a distant city are still on (Le Monde S'endort's own register), including one window burning distinctly warmer than the rest (Midnight Work's one lit window, carried this far by the Global Art Direction's own world-activity/local-memory doctrine, §10).
- **Humans:** none — New Signal's human density stays `SPARSE` throughout (Global Art Direction §4), and this frame has none.
- **Activity:** a retreating storm (Hold The Light's own register) still holds the inland sky in grey, visibly moving out to sea behind the vehicle; ahead, the first gold light catches the headland and the distant beach.
- **Material:** wet road surface after the passing storm, holding a brief, unstable reflection (Telatelaba's mirror register, echoed rather than literally staged) that shows both the retreating grey storm-light behind and the gold ahead in the same puddle, side by side, never blended into one. Weathered stone at the road's edge carries the era's **silver** half of the "gold and silver" pairing (`OWNER_DIRECT`).
- **Light:** the frame's one true convergence point — gold ahead (the sun clearing the storm), the storm's own cool grey-blue still holding behind, and the distant city's small warm point along the shoreline's curve — three light qualities visible at three different distances in one continuous, naturally graded view, never stacked in the foreground.
- **Weather:** the storm is still visibly present but retreating — onshore wind carrying the last of the rain out to sea.
- **Vehicle relationship:** stopped at the overlook, engine presumably off — a pause for the view, distinct from Étééaooété's own arrival-at-rest at the beach itself.
- **Density:** `VARIABLE`, lower here than the sub-arc-1 register, consistent with contamination decreasing through sub-arc 3 — but not yet at Étééaooété's own lowest point, since the beach itself is only glimpsed, not yet reached.
- **Anomaly:** dormant — nothing here is this frame's own central anomaly; this card is explicitly the era's *overview* frame, not any one track's own signature moment.
- **Lambda treatment:** not staged in this frame at all — consistent with the doctrine's "increasingly recognizable, not yet ordinary" stage for this point in the era (Global Art Direction §11). Étééaooété's own frame remains the doctrine's "ordinary world-form" culmination, not this one.
- **New Signal guardrail (canonical, owner-ratified, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` final reconciliation) — `ACCEPT WITH GUARDRAIL`:**
  1. One real geography must dominate every New Signal frame.
  2. Other worlds may appear only as reflection, light, signal, distant silhouette, weather, material, trace, or brief memory — never as a second fully-realized geography competing for attention.
  3. No literal accumulation of track landmarks in one frame.
  4. No visual checklist of the era's own journey.
  5. No museum-like final panorama.
  6. Étééaooété must remain radically simple in perception even when technically complex.

  **This frame complies by construction, not by exception:** the coastal overlook itself is the one dominant geography (rule 1). Every "other world" element maps onto exactly one allowed category from rule 2 — the storm is **weather**, the distant city is **distant silhouette**, its one window is **light**, the wet-road effect is **reflection** — none is staged as its own competing place. Nothing here is a landmark object placed for recognition (rule 3), the frame is one continuous graded view, not an inventory (rule 4), and it is explicitly *not* Étééaooété's own final panorama (rule 5) — that track's own frame, and its own radical-simplicity requirement (rule 6), are untouched and still its own. The previously-flagged tension against Étééaooété's "no accumulation" guardrail is resolved by this reading, not merely asserted — each element's category is named above precisely so it can be checked.
