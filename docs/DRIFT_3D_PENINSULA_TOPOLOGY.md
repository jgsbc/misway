# MISWAY DRIFT — Peninsula World Topology

**Status:** spatial north star for the current Fable world-building phase.

This document is intentionally concise. It is not a backlog, a readiness dossier, a governance artefact, or a replacement for the canonical era contracts, track atlas, cue maps, Realism Bible, or accepted WebP masterframes.

Its purpose is to keep the global map coherent while `/drift-greybox-lab` develops freely and `/drift` remains the production convergence target.

---

## 1. Core thesis

DRIFT should not become:

- one long corridor hidden under decorative bends;
- five disconnected biome islands;
- an empty open world;
- twenty-six roadside track stops;
- one reusable scene template recoloured for every era.

The strongest global form is:

> **A semi-open road movie across a folded peninsula organised around one central massif, with a compact traversal spine and era-specific lateral territories, loops, detours, vertical routes and temporal transformations.**

The massif gives unity.  
The eras give different spatial grammars.  
The tracks transform the lived world.

---

## 2. Authority boundaries

The following remain authoritative:

- `entry.webp`
- `birth-yard.webp`
- `older-shadows.webp`
- `vegetative-field.webp`
- `new-signal.webp`
- canonical era contracts;
- canonical track identities and emotional meanings;
- accepted cue maps and audio authority;
- the Realism Bible;
- the sand safari 4x4 identity.

This topology document governs **global spatial coherence and traversal logic**.

It does not prescribe final art detail, exact coordinates, final road length, final cue timing, or mandatory implementation order.

The current one-dimensional `z` spine and current track coordinates are useful blockout anchors, not final geography.

## 3. Global geography

The world is one folded peninsula whose terrain explains the sequence of eras.

```text
                              NORTH

                       OLDER SHADOWS
                    massif / cols / ridges
                  branches / climbs / descents
                       /                 \
                      /                   \
       ENTRY ---- BIRTH YARD          VEGETATIVE FIELD
       cliff cave    estuary-port      wet suburban basin
       compression   dense network     loops / repetition
            \                             /
             \                           /
              -------- central bay -----
                           \
                            NEW SIGNAL
                      coast / headlands / ocean
                      reused places / final beach
```

Physical logic:

```text
snow, rain and springs from the massif
→ valleys and retention basins
→ Birth Yard canals and estuary
→ central bay
→ New Signal ocean
```

Emotional logic:

```text
confinement
→ collective pressure
→ expansion, risk and altitude
→ comfortable repetition and flattening
→ chosen reopening toward the horizon
```

From New Signal, distant Birth Yard lights may be visible across the bay at very low cost. The world visually closes without becoming a forced gameplay loop.

---

## 4. Three navigation layers

### 4.1 Compact traversal spine

Keep a short, continuous route capable of crossing all five eras quickly.

It is used for:

- orientation;
- rapid traversal;
- world integration testing;
- continuity between eras;
- access to major territories;
- stable streaming behaviour.

It must be folded through curves, altitude changes, occlusion and long views. It must not read as one straight `z` axis.

### 4.2 Era-specific expansions

Each era opens away from the spine according to its own identity:

- **Entry:** one directed passage;
- **Birth Yard:** urban network around water;
- **Older Shadows:** branches, climbs, descents and plateaus;
- **Vegetative Field:** loops, false repetitions and cul-de-sacs;
- **New Signal:** a coastal constellation of a few strong places revisited by several tracks.

### 4.3 Track transformations

A track can be one of four forms:

| Form | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Place** | a physically distinct territory |
| **Detour** | a branch, loop, ascent or optional route |
| **Event** | a temporal transformation of an existing place |
| **State** | light, weather, activity, population or memory affecting one or several territories |

Not every track needs a new district. The map should avoid twenty-six mini-levels.

---

## 5. Entry — directed threshold

Entry remains short, compressed and irreversible.

```text
birth chamber
→ mineral gorge
→ incongruous administrative relay
→ rising pressure
→ deep fractured Λ breach
→ ledge reveal
→ Birth Yard
```

Rules:

- no secondary roads;
- no open-world behaviour;
- no floating logo;
- the Λ impact comes from rock depth, parallax, falling material, projected light, atmosphere and sound;
- the breach should frame several Birth Yard depths before the vehicle fully exits.

Entry is the only era whose strength comes from refusing choice.

---

## 6. Birth Yard — continuous port organism

Birth Yard is one dense, violent, dirty and inhabited port city organised around a real estuary or basin.

The water is structural geography, not a decorative plane.

Required physical truths:

- a readable opposite bank;
- credible quay walls and water level;
- no buildings unintentionally standing inside the basin;
- no vehicle appearing to drive on dry ground through water;
- bridges, vessels and port infrastructure belong to the geography;
- streets, canals, quays and courtyards connect into one organism.

Conceptual arrangement:

```text
                         PLAY IT
                  functional district
                           |
       FOOLFOULE --- mooring yard --- EUX GAINENT
       dense canyon       |           cold waterfront display
            |             |
       JAZZYPLING     lifting bridge
       alleys/cellars     |
            \------ A WALK IN ZEELAND
                    outer quay / canal / horizon
```

Track roles:

- **A WALK IN ZEELAND:** outer quay, canal, wind, solitude, sunset distance and absent-life reflections;
- **FOOLFOULE:** compressed commercial canyon, crowd pressure, flux and signs reacting to movement;
- **JAZZYPLING:** alleys, stairs, basements, marginal nightlife and unsafe curiosity;
- **PLAY IT:** offices, transport, parking, duty, repetition and social script;
- **EUX GAINENT:** the cold working-body display facing the basin, tied physically to port or logistics infrastructure.

The mooring yard remains an accepted era-level landmark. It may be visible from several territories and transformed by tracks, but it must not erase canonical track signatures.

Birth Yard is spatially continuous, but its track territories must be unmistakably different through morphology, circulation, light, sound, activity and relationship to water.

---

## 7. Older Shadows — massif with several faces

Older Shadows is not a narrow mountain corridor. It is the era where the world opens.

Conceptual arrangement:

```text
                           RISE
                         summit
                           |
             BLOSSOMING -- ridge / speed
                    \      |
                     \     |
                 ETHNIC STICK
                  warm plateau
                         \
                    MINUIT MOINS CINQ
                       real pass / choice
                              \
                             PERDUE
                         fogged descent
```

Spatial principles:

- scale comes from horizon, height and branch choice, not empty road length;
- the player can see destinations before reaching them;
- routes differ by altitude, exposure, risk and surface;
- the warm African/root dimension of ETHNIC STICK emerges through a credible change of slope, geology, climate and human presence, not a pasted biome;
- MINUIT MOINS CINQ is a real topographic decision point;
- PERDUE gradually removes landmarks, visibility and shared presence while descending toward the next era.

Representative grammars:

- climb;
- ridge;
- descent;
- plateau;
- detour;
- return route.

---

## 8. Vegetative Field — compact labyrinth of comfort

Vegetative Field is not a jungle.

It is a wet, grey, repetitive suburban basin whose apparent comfort slowly becomes spatial and emotional enclosure.

Conceptual arrangement:

```text
                   MORNE, ET ?
                  grey centre
                 /          \
            DAYMASON       TIME
         unfinished area   repetition loop
                 \          /
                    CHAILK
                 cleared void
                       |
                   TANTITOM
               colour and exit
```

Spatial principles:

- use detached houses, wet asphalt, clipped hedges, retention basins, minor equipment, schools, small shops, roundabouts and cul-de-sacs;
- increase perceived size through loops and near-repetition rather than physical length;
- roads may return close to places already crossed;
- junctions should be readable enough to drive yet difficult to remember perfectly;
- repeated forms must vary subtly in wear, garden life, occupancy and light;
- CHAILK acts as a meaningful empty reset space;
- TANTITOM progressively restores colour, irregular behaviour and openness.

The exit from Vegetative Field should be strong: after familiar repeated streets, a short rise or break reveals the ocean.

## 9. New Signal — one coast, several readings

New Signal should not become eleven consecutive districts or a collage of prior worlds.

It should contain approximately five strong coastal places transformed by several tracks.

### 9.1 Promontory road

Tracks:

- NEEKTAREUM
- ASITIS
- RELATIVE

The same geography shifts from wounded agency, to acceptance, to a changed point of view.

### 9.2 Signal ridge

Tracks:

- OVERTHINK
- HOLD THE LIGHT

Infrastructure, wind, lights and lines move from overload to the deliberate preservation of one fragile source.

### 9.3 Nocturnal house and distant town

Tracks:

- MIDNIGHT WORK
- TELATELABA
- LE MONDE S’ENDORT

A small set of places changes through work, family memory, distance, silence and night.

### 9.4 Coastal quarry and dark wood

Tracks:

- RENEE SENS
- PANTHÈRE

Worked matter, geological time and nocturnal instinct share one physical territory without becoming symbolic props.

### 9.5 Terminal beach and bay

Track:

- ÉTÉÉAOOÉTÉ

Required geography:

- a real bay or ocean-facing beach;
- visible headlands or opposite land where geographically appropriate;
- convincing water depth and shoreline;
- immense waves;
- a ritual integrated into bodies, surf, wind and space;
- no floating Lambda or explicit emblem.

The final beach may feel conclusive without becoming a sealed ending. A coastline or route may continue beyond sight.

## 10. Era transitions must be geographic

Transitions are not primarily fades, fog tunnels or colour changes.

They should emerge from believable edge territories.

### Birth Yard → Older Shadows

```text
working port
→ warehouses
→ industrial fringe
→ quarry / retaining walls
→ first slopes
→ mountain road
```

### Older Shadows → Vegetative Field

```text
fogged valley
→ managed forest
→ retaining walls
→ first detached houses
→ roundabout
→ suburban basin
```

### Vegetative Field → New Signal

```text
repetitive street
→ underpass or short climb
→ ridge line
→ sudden ocean reveal
```

These transitions carry traces of both neighbouring eras without becoming hybrid theme parks.

---

## 11. World memory

The world should not reset cleanly between tracks.

Useful persistent traces include:

- lights left on;
- moved vehicles or objects;
- changed crowd distribution;
- wet or drying surfaces;
- altered port activity;
- a bridge left in another position;
- a previously active building now dark;
- residual anomaly behaviour;
- a distant signal visible later from another era.

Memory should be selective and legible. It is not a requirement to simulate everything.

---

## 12. Navigation principles

The player drives physically. The road never secretly drives the player.

The map guides through:

- dominant silhouettes;
- horizon lines;
- road surface and width;
- light;
- sound propagation;
- infrastructure;
- visible destinations;
- credible barriers and topography.

Desired navigation:

```text
clear main route
+ seductive detours
+ simple returns
+ no mandatory minimap
```

Desktop, touch and gamepad controls must preserve acceleration, braking, reverse, steering and zoom without auto-propulsion or strong hidden route following.

---

## 13. Topology implementation direction

The current longitudinal blockout may remain while the world is proven.

Do not rewrite everything at once.

Progressively evolve topology from `z` intervals toward a graph of regions and routes.

Illustrative shape:

```ts
export type DriftWorldRegion = {
  id: string;
  eraId: string;
  polygon: Array<{ x: number; z: number }>;
  altitudeRange: [number, number];
  entryRouteIds: string[];
  exitRouteIds: string[];
  trackIds: string[];
  distantLandmarkIds: string[];
};

export type DriftRouteSegment = {
  id: string;
  fromRegionId: string;
  toRegionId: string;
  spline: Array<{ x: number; y: number; z: number }>;
  kind: "spine" | "branch" | "loop" | "service";
  surfaceId: string;
};

export type DriftTrackTerritory = {
  trackId: string;
  regionId: string;
  form: "place" | "detour" | "event" | "state";
  bounds?: Array<{ x: number; z: number }>;
  approachRouteIds?: string[];
  temporalStateIds?: string[];
};
```

The exact API may differ. The important change is conceptual:

- regions instead of only longitudinal ranges;
- route graph instead of one axis;
- track form instead of mandatory physical district;
- neighbouring-region streaming instead of only `z` distance.

---

## 14. Streaming and visual continuity

At runtime, prefer three levels:

1. active region at full intended quality;
2. neighbouring or visible regions at simplified quality;
3. distant dominant silhouettes at very low cost.

Examples of distant continuity:

- Birth Yard cranes visible from lower mountain roads;
- Older Shadows summit visible from Vegetative Field;
- suburban lights visible below New Signal;
- Birth Yard glow visible across the final bay.

Do not load every detailed territory simultaneously.

Do not let important geography visibly pop out of existence.

---

## 15. Fast development sequence

The map should now progress through structure before detail.

Recommended order:

1. preserve the current compact spine and working controls;
2. fold the spine into a coherent peninsula plan;
3. close Birth Yard around a real basin and create meaningful connections;
4. add at least two real route choices in Older Shadows;
5. create one genuine loop in Vegetative Field;
6. reshape New Signal into a coastal constellation of reusable places;
7. verify large views, transitions, water boundaries and recovery routes;
8. keep intermediate geometry, atmosphere and strong materials;
9. return to track detail only after the map itself is compelling to traverse.

The existing Entry → Birth Yard hero slice remains the local quality reference. Its full detail cost must not be replicated across the entire world during topology construction.

---

## 16. Anti-patterns

Reject:

- a straight route disguised with visual bends;
- five adjacent biomes without shared geology;
- twenty-six mandatory roadside landmarks;
- one topology reused for every era;
- empty distance used as fake scale;
- transitions made only from fog or colour;
- an alpine mountain next to a pasted African scene;
- Vegetative Field as wild vegetation;
- New Signal as a museum of prior eras;
- water without shoreline logic;
- basins without opposite banks;
- road surfaces appearing inside water;
- detailed foreground art multiplied before streaming and distance treatment exist.

---

## 17. Success condition

The topology succeeds when:

- the complete world is recognisable as one peninsula;
- each era changes the way the player moves, not only what the player sees;
- the compact spine remains useful without containing every experience;
- tracks alternate between places, detours, events and states;
- large views create memory and orientation;
- water, altitude, roads and settlements form credible geography;
- the world feels larger than its loaded geometry;
- the player wants to leave the main route without becoming lost in empty space;
- `/drift-greybox-lab` can explore freely while proven systems remain promotable into `/drift`.

> **Keep the spine as an instrument. Fold it into a peninsula. Let each era choose its own movement grammar. Build the world before polishing every metre.**
