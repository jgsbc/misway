# DRIFT 3D — Birth Yard Spatial Graph

**Lot:** `DRIFT-SPATIAL-BY-00`  
**Status:** `PROPOSED — OWNER / LAB GATE REQUIRED`  
**Parent authority:** `docs/DRIFT_3D_SPATIAL_BIBLE.md`

## 1. Purpose

This lot resolves the first open item in the Canonical Spatial Bible: establish one coherent road graph for Birth Yard's seven active tracks **before moving any runtime node or rebuilding any territory**.

It is intentionally a graph/design lot, not a world-rebuild lot.

It does not:

- move a track node;
- change terrain, water, colliders or vehicle physics;
- promote Jazzypling to production;
- implement the three proposed new route links in `/drift-evolution`;
- redefine any track anomaly, signature, cue or musical meaning.

The graph reuses current anchors and the strongest spatial evidence already in the repo.

## 2. Governing idea

Birth Yard should read as **one dense port city with several urban faces**, not seven dioramas connected by empty road.

The graph therefore uses:

1. one mandatory first reveal through Zeeland;
2. one five-track urban loop;
3. two purposeful short spurs;
4. no duplicate harbour system;
5. no new global road engine.

```text
                           FUNKY HOO
                           dock/night spur
                                |
                                |
ENTRY ──> A WALK IN ZEELAND ──> FOOLFOULE ──> SUGARED PEACH
               ^                    |                 |
               |                    |                 |
               |                    +-----------------+
               |                                      |
               |                                      v
               +────────── JAZZYPLING <────────── PLAY IT
                                |
                                |
                           PEUT-ETRE
                     buried-city fringe spur
```

The drawn diagonal from Foolfoule toward Sugared Peach is the continuation of the commercial canyon, not an extra branch. The loop is:

```text
A WALK IN ZEELAND
→ FOOLFOULE
→ SUGARED PEACH
→ PLAY IT
→ JAZZYPLING
→ A WALK IN ZEELAND
```

Funky Hoo and Peut-être are deliberate spurs because their spatial identities benefit from a destination-like edge condition rather than being forced into the circulation loop.

## 4. Mandatory Entry relation

Entry remains a threshold, not a road choice.

The player exits the accepted cave and is first exposed to **A WALK IN ZEELAND**. There is no direct Entry bypass to Peut-être, Funky Hoo, Jazzypling or any other Birth Yard track.

This preserves the accepted logic:

```text
confinement
→ water / horizon / first city exposure
→ choice and compression
```

Zeeland is therefore both a track territory and Birth Yard's spatial vestibule.

## 5. Main urban loop

### 5.1 Zeeland → Foolfoule — `production_promoted`

Keep the accepted dry east-bank route and harbour relationship exactly as current production authority defines them.

Spatial job:

- Zeeland opens the world;
- Foolfoule compresses it;
- the harbour remains readable behind/alongside the approach;
- the route never drives through canal or basin water.

No redesign in this lot.

### 5.2 Foolfoule → Sugared Peach — `proposed`

Use Foolfoule's **existing east mouth** as the natural release from crowd/commercial pressure.

Sugared Peach should become a **small irregular street pocket / widening**, not a circus, stage or separate plaza-world.

Spatial job:

- pressure visibly relaxes over a short distance;
- buildings step down slightly;
- enough lateral room exists for the hopping brass presence without blocking the drive corridor;
- the humour is encountered as ordinary street absurdity.

The existing Sugared Peach node at `(-48, 46)` is already only ~15 m from Foolfoule and is therefore a strong reuse-first candidate.

### 5.3 Sugared Peach → Play It — `proposed`

The route leaves the irregular brass pocket and becomes progressively more ordered.

Spatial job:

- street markings become more regular;
- façades align;
- crossings/metro/business infrastructure begin before the Play It center;
- the transition must feel like urban organization tightening, not a teleport into an office diorama.

The current Play It anchor `(-54, 26)` remains the candidate center.

### 5.4 Play It → Jazzypling — `proposed tie-in to existing lab proof`

This is the vertical social underside of the same city:

```text
commuter/business order
→ service/back street
→ crooked cellar district
```

Reuse the existing Jazzypling lab route endpoint around `(-57.4, 20.8)` as the first candidate tie-in rather than inventing another approach.

The final few metres to Play It need a lab route proof; they are not declared delivered here.

### 5.5 Jazzypling → Zeeland — `lab_implemented`

Preserve the existing lab principle:

- the crooked cellar route branches from Zeeland's southern dry junction;
- the route crosses the Jazzypling center;
- it remains a district, not a closed arena;
- it reconnects Birth Yard rather than becoming an isolated dead end.

This closes the five-track main loop once the Play It tie-in is implemented.

## 6. Funky Hoo — west-quay night spur

### Decision proposed

Funky Hoo should **reuse Zeeland's harbour geography**, not create another canal/harbour system.

Its current anchor `(-96, 40)` is kept as the proposal because it naturally sits on the west side of the accepted Zeeland/harbour composition.

The route branches from the Zeeland harbour circulation and uses the **existing lifting-bridge/quay language** as its connector.

### Spatial identity

Funky Hoo is the same port city after dark, seen from the working/nightlife edge:

- wetter pavement;
- brick frontage;
- loading/service spaces;
- restrained red-window register;
- harbour air and industrial residue;
- enough road width for a believable turnaround at the spur end.

### Anti-duplication rule

Funky Hoo must not become:

- a second Zeeland;
- a second canal network;
- an Amsterdam postcard;
- a red-light theme park;
- a separate water authority.

Zeeland owns the water geography. Funky Hoo borrows that geography and changes **activity, density, frontage and night register**.

## 7. Peut-être — southern buried-city fringe spur

### Decision proposed

Peut-être remains in Birth Yard but belongs on its **southern urban fringe**, where city fabric can plausibly be overtaken by windblown sand, spoil and partial burial.

The current anchor `(-96, 0)` is retained as the graph proposal.

It branches from the Jazzypling/southern-city side rather than directly from Entry. Entry therefore cannot skip Zeeland.

### Spatial identity

The scene should read as:

- a real urban boulevard/service edge first;
- recognizable civic/Parisian geometry partially buried second;
- sand as accumulated matter, not an instant desert biome;
- a quieter, more open edge than central Birth Yard.

This is how the track can remain visibly Birth Yard while carrying its future-Paris image.

### Era-transition note

Peut-être is a **candidate** location for a later southbound handoff toward Older Shadows because its decreasing urban density can support a transition. `DRIFT-SPATIAL-BY-00` does not decide that inter-era route.

## 8. Why Sugared Peach sits between Foolfoule and Play It

This placement is not based only on coordinate convenience.

It creates a useful pressure rhythm:

```text
Foolfoule
commercial compression / crowd pressure

→ Sugared Peach
brief physical and comic release

→ Play It
order, rules, crossings, commuter discipline
```

That makes Sugared Peach a small but structurally useful urban beat. It should not be enlarged into a major district merely because it has a track node.

## 11. Driveability constraints for the next lab lot

The graph is accepted only as a design hypothesis until `/drift-evolution` proves the proposed edges with the actual selected vehicle.

The next implementation must verify:

1. no new road crosses Zeeland structural water without a real bridge/quay crossing;
2. the Defender can take every bend without clipping buildings or crowd zones;
3. each proposed route maintains useful camera clearance;
4. the Foolfoule → Sugared transition does not collide with Foolfoule's east building masses;
5. the Sugared pocket leaves the drive lane open;
6. Play It has a readable approach before the node center;
7. the Jazzypling tie-in preserves the cellar district rather than widening it into a generic boulevard;
8. Funky Hoo has a credible turnaround and does not require a new harbour basin;
9. Peut-être's sand does not become a collider/water-style authority error and does not capture the Entry cave;
10. every route remains inside world/movement bounds.
