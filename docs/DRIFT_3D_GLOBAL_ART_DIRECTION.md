**Effective:** upon merge of `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40`.
**Relationship to existing authorities:** this document adds nothing that contradicts `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md` (visual authority) or `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` (artistic/narrative/behavioral doctrine). ## 1. Global narrative

Drift is MISWAY's playable road movie through a mental and autobiographical world — a listening world, not a racing game, not a decorative music catalogue, not a literal diary.

Two founding statements exist and are **complementary, not competing** (`DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` itself reconciles them: *"la seconde met en scène la première ; elle ne la remplace pas"*):

- **Meaning** (`DRIFT_3D_INTEGRAL_PACKAGE_ADOPTION.md`, founding decision #1): *"MISWAY Drift est un road movie musical à travers un monde mental qui s'est organisé pour continuer à fonctionner après une rupture, révélant peu à peu ce qu'il a dû sacrifier pour tenir debout."*
- **Mode of revelation** (`DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §1.2): *"Tout paraît normal jusqu'à ce qu'un détail révèle que la logique du monde est fausse."*

The world is inspired by a real trajectory but **never becomes a literal diary** (Living World Bible §2.3) — no personal data, medical detail, identifiable score-settling, real-person caricature, family exposure, or over-explained symbol.

## 2. Visual realism doctrine — the global ratio

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §3.1 only.** The Realism Bible states *how* to build credible reality (PBR, human scale, physical light, color script) but does not itself state a numeric ratio — the ratio is a Living World Bible doctrine that the Realism Bible's principles serve.

> **70% recognizable credible reality — 20% poetic/emotional (graphic) distortion — 10% absurd/uncanny (impossible) anomaly.**

This is the reference ratio (it matches the Birth Yard era exactly). *"Cette proportion évolue selon les ères"* — it is a center of gravity, not a per-frame quota:

| Era | Reality | Distortion | Impossible |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | 90% | — | 10% ("folie visible") |
| Birth Yard | 70% | 20% | 10% |
| Older Shadows | 75% | 15% | 10% |
| Vegetative Field | 85% baseline, **up to 55% contamination after the system breaks down** | — | — |
| New Signal | 45–65% (varies by track) | 25–40% | 10–20% |

**Governing test** (Realism Bible): *"Un inconnu qui voit un screenshot doit pouvoir dire ce que c'est en une phrase."* The reality floor exists so the absurd anomaly reads as **one credible thing behaving wrongly**, never as an abstract or cartoon space. This is why the absurd axis is described as "through one credible anomaly emerging from reality, not through cartoon clutter" — the 10% never gets its own separate unreal geometry; it erupts from the 70%.

## 3. Humor and absurdity doctrine

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §4.2 only** (not covered elsewhere).

Tone: *"sec, visuel, décalé, parfois tendre et rarement explicatif."*

**Allowed families**: functional inversion; impossible bureaucracy; empty performance; delayed reaction; false scale; excessive repetition; rebellious object; deadpan; creative self-irony.

**Forbidden**: pasted meme; constant textual joke; crudeness as sole device; total cynicism; cultural caricature; punching down; a gag unrelated to the track.

Restraint governs delivery (§4.1): *"la scène ne montre pas toutes ses idées en même temps."* EUX GAINENT's own Identity Contract is the working example of this register in practice: *"No villain appears. The unease comes from a process that works perfectly while quietly changing what 'gain' means"* — deadpan/bureaucratic, never slapstick, matching this doctrine exactly (`CONFIRMED` alignment, no conflict).

## 4. Density — global doctrine (added by owner decision, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` decision session)

Previously `UNDER-SPECIFIED` — no active document used "density" as a defined rule with numeric targets across the whole world; only each Era Contract's own qualitative level (`SPARSE`/`LOW`/`MEDIUM`/`HIGH`/`VERY_HIGH`/`VARIABLE`) existed. The owner required this gap closed. This section is therefore net-new authorship reconciling those existing per-era levels into one doctrine, not an extraction from any prior source.

Density is not a single number — it is a per-era **target band** on the same five-point scale the Track Atlas already uses per segment, applied independently across four axes: **foreground** (within ~20m of the drivable corridor), **background** (massing/skyline/silhouette count beyond that), **human presence** (§9), and **behavioral loops** (autonomous objects with a perceptible cycle). A segment's overall density is the combination of its four axis values, never a single average.

**Per-era target bands** (reconciling the Atlas's own existing per-track density notes into one era-level statement):

| Era | Foreground | Background | Human presence | Behavioral loops |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | `SPARSE` | `SPARSE` | `SPARSE` (0) | `SPARSE` |
| Birth Yard | `VERY_HIGH` | `VERY_HIGH` | `VERY_HIGH` | `MEDIUM` |
| Older Shadows | `MEDIUM` | `HIGH` (mountain massing) | `LOW` (except Ethnic Stick's warm crowd) | `LOW` |
| Vegetative Field | `MEDIUM` | `MEDIUM` (repetitive, not sparse) | `LOW` | `HIGH` (machines carry the era's life) |
| New Signal | `VARIABLE` | `VARIABLE` | `SPARSE` throughout | `HIGH`-then-purged in sub-arc 1, decreasing to `SPARSE` by sub-arc 3 |

**The one invariant rule, unchanged at every target band**: density fills the world's *edges and stillness*, never the *driving lane* — corridors and node protection are a surviving `DRIFT_3D_SET_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md` gameplay rule (§13 below), not a density exception at any band.

**Still genuinely open**: the exact numeric population counts a Quality Tier scales from/to per axis per era remain a Build-lot decision, not a doctrine-level one — this section states the *target band*, never the *implementation number*.

- **Behavioral budget** (Living World Bible §13.2): 1 active signature situation maximum; 2–4 secondary loops; 1 dominant animated media surface maximum; 1–2 active nearby canvas textures maximum; off-screen events disabled.
- **Physical budget** (Realism Bible and Living World Bible §13.1, same numbers): ≤300 visible draw calls/zone (instancing + atlas), ≤1.5M triangles, 1K prop / 2K hero textures, KTX2, 3-level LOD.

## 5. Scale conventions

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md` only** (principle 4).

Doors ~2.1m, floors ~3m, streetlights ~4m, lanes ~3.5m — proportioned to vehicle and camera. Every point of interest must be identifiable by silhouette at 200m.

## 6. Color doctrine

- **Realism Bible** (principle 5): color script is scripted per track, day/night cycle authored, *"la lumière et la couleur portent l'émotion avant le nombre d'assets."*
- **`DRIFT_3D_COLOR_SCRIPT.md`** is the executable table (one row per track: hour/weather/palette/emotion/test-phrase) — Step 1 of the Realism Bible's method. Runtime lighting interpolates along the route; no hard cut outside scripted transitions. New Signal's "silver" segments carry no warm diegetic source; "gold" segments concentrate their warmth on exactly one source.
- **Living World Bible** §6.4: *"La couleur suit le color script existant."* The contamination layer (the 20%) uses one strong accent color, an optional secondary, black/white/raw material, and misregistration/partial flat fills — never a second independent palette.
- **Forbidden** (both documents state this; not a conflict, a reinforced rule): global rainbow; uniform saturation; identical palette across tracks; unsourced neon; arbitrary decorative gradients; emissive color without a diegetic source.
- **New Signal's gold/silver pairing** (owner-confirmed, added by owner decision, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` decision session): New Signal's color statement is gold **and** silver, not gold alone. Gold segments concentrate their warmth on exactly one source (the existing Color Script rule, restated here for New Signal specifically); silver segments carry no warm diegetic source at all — cool, reflective, desaturated matter (wet sand, worn stone, still water, glass) stands in contrast to gold's warmth rather than competing with it. Neither is decorative: gold marks a source-driven event (a signature moment, a sunrise), silver marks a material property (something worn, reflective, or memory-bearing) — the two never blend into a single warm-cool gradient wash.

## 7. Lighting doctrine

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md`** (principle 1 + absolute prohibitions + post-processing).

One coherent sun/moon source per segment plus justified local sources. ACES tonemapping, per-zone exposure, shadows consistent with the scripted hour. *"Une lumière vient toujours de quelque chose : lampadaire, fenêtre, feu, lune."* One directional light per segment + baked AO/light probes; point lights limited and diegetic. Post-processing stays sober: light bloom (high threshold), SSAO, soft vignette, fine grain, contemplative DOF, one LUT per zone. Exposure adapts over 2–3s to major changes (e.g. exiting a cave).

---

## 8. Materials doctrine

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md` only** (principle 2).

Photo-sourced PBR materials (Poly Haven or equivalent): albedo + roughness + normal, dirt/puddle/wear/rust decals. **Never a flat color without a texture.** Prefer reworked free photorealistic libraries (Poly Haven materials/HDRI, adapted CC models) over abstract modeling. No mesh ships without a textured PBR material.

## 9. Human presence — unified doctrine (added by owner decision, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` decision session)

Previously `UNDER-SPECIFIED` as a unified doctrine — human-presence rules lived only as scattered per-zone notes in the Realism Bible (Birth Yard: *"foule instanciée, trafic, vapeur, linge aux fenêtres"*; foolfoule: *"foule compacte au pas mécanique, visages neutres: des robots"*; **eux-gainent explicitly forbids literal robots**: *"silhouettes humaines au mouvement mécanique... aucun robot littéral"*; ethnic stick: *"la seule foule chaleureuse"*). The owner required these reconciled into one rule. This section is net-new authorship unifying those existing notes, not an extraction of a rule that already existed in this form.

1. **Legibility**: every human reads through silhouette, timing and comparative behavior only — never face, costume label, or dialogue. This is the EUX GAINENT Identity Contract's A/B/C treatment, generalized to every era.
2. **Register, not literal machinery**: a crowd may read as *mechanical in behavior* (Foolfoule's own "almost-robotic crowd," owner-confirmed) but a track may never depict literal robots standing in for humans — EUX GAINENT's own guardrail against this, generalized here as a world-wide prohibition, not a track-local one.
3. **Density follows §4's per-era target band** — human presence is one of the four density axes above, never authored independently of it.
4. **Warmth is scarce and named where it exists**: only Ethnic Stick is on record as carrying "the only warm crowd" in its era (Older Shadows) — elsewhere, human presence trends toward incidental, functional, or absent, and this asymmetry is deliberate, not an oversight.
5. **The twelve silent recurring archetypes** (late employee, person waiting, child following the invisible, and nine more not yet named) remain the one human-presence fact this doctrine cannot yet complete (`DRIFT_3D_INTEGRAL_PACKAGE_ADOPTION.md` founding decision #4, approved by title and count only) — a future lot must locate or author the remaining nine before this list is build-ready. **This specific gap survives this correction unresolved, named rather than invented** — the owner's rework request was for the doctrine that governs human presence, not for the missing archetype names themselves.

This doctrine does not reopen any track's own Identity Contract or Era Contract population note — it states the shared rule those future contracts must each specialize, never override.

## 10. World activity / autonomous life

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §7, §8, §10, §12.**

- Every significant object holds at least two of six roles: physical, narrative, media, musical, interactive, memorial (§7.1–7.2: Diffuser, Actor, Instrument, Trigger, Memory, Anomaly).
- An object has at most three states: normal; contaminated; resolved-or-silent (§7.4).
- Trigger priority order (§7.5): proximity → musical phase → stillness → collision → orientation/framing → headlights → return → sequence.
- Local memory (§10.1–10.2): the world remembers nodes visited, tracks activated, events seen, objects hit, recent trajectories, order of passage, hidden interactions — no counter is required. Manifestations: a mirror replaying a trajectory, a transformed trace, a modified poster, a light recognizing the vehicle, a wave returning an old motif, a crowd avoiding the player, a sign changing tone.
- World sound (§12): diegetic layer + behavioral layer; world sound lowers as the track becomes dominant.

## 11. Lambda (λ) usage

**Confirmed source: `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md` + `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §6.5, identical wording, no conflict — the construction rule below is unchanged by this correction.**

λ = the door, and "lambda" = the ordinary/anonymous. It is **always sculpted into the set**, never floating or self-luminous: the entry cave's mouth, inverted-Y forks (minuit moins cinq, neektareum), framing/timberwork, cracks, cast shadows, negative space, foam, traces, alignment, crowd movement. *"Il doit être découvert, produit, sculpté, cadré ou activé. Jamais simplement ajouté."* Locked decision (Living World Bible §18): *"Le λ est produit par le décor."*

This is also the origin of "lambda-world" as this project's word for *ordinary, personal, strange, universal* (the retired `DRIFT_3D_ART_DIRECTION.md`'s own phrase, still true in spirit even though its visual language is superseded).

**Narrative-weight progression across the five eras** (owner-decided, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` decision session — this section reworks and replaces this lot's own first proposal on the same subject, per explicit owner correction, noted below rather than silently overwritten):

1. **Entry — exceptional threshold.** The one light source in near-total darkness, the sole admission. Maximum rarity and charge.
2. **Birth Yard / Older Shadows / Vegetative Field — rare/embedded.** No confirmed λ appearance exists in current per-track material for these three eras. This is a fact about current documentation coverage, not a claim about the world's own psychological state — **an earlier draft of this doctrine stated that "the world is not yet ready to recognize its own door"; that interpretive claim was unsupported by any source and is withdrawn by explicit owner decision.** Where a future Identity Contract does introduce λ in one of these eras, it stays embedded and singular, consistent with Entry's own register — not yet a recurring form.
3. **New Signal, sub-arcs 1–2 — increasingly recognizable.** λ begins to recur (Asitis's three-crack λ) without yet being commonplace — each appearance is still a discrete, load-bearing event a player might miss.
4. **New Signal, sub-arc 3 — ordinary world-form.** By Panthere (a shadow-trajectory λ, visible only from one angle) and especially Étééaooété (an entire λ-composition assembled from the beach's own ordinary matter — foam, wood, stone, tire-tracks), λ has become a shape the world itself produces routinely, not a rare event. Étééaooété's own signature is the doctrine's clearest expression: technically the most complex moment in the catalogue, narratively required to read as radically simple — complexity in service of ordinariness, never spectacle.

**What never changes across this progression**: the construction rule above (sculpted, never floating, never simply added) and the no-decorative-repetition rule — even at its most "ordinary" in New Signal, every λ appearance stays individually justified by its own scene, never a repeated stamp or logo.

## 12. Transitions

The only fully-specified transition remains the cave crossing into Birth Yard (Realism Bible): concrete → rock, dirty grey fog → clear blue, with a 2–3s eye-adaptation "burn" on crossing. Runtime lighting interpolates continuously along the route; **never a hard cut outside a scripted transition** (Color Script, reinforced by Realism Bible). No active document specifies the Older Shadows → Vegetative Field or Vegetative Field → New Signal transition *content* beyond each era's own mood description — the four Era Contracts each own their own outbound transition, and this specific content gap is **not** closed by this section (a future Era Contract revision, not a global-doctrine matter).

**Era-transition principles** (added by owner decision, `DRIFT-IV-GOV-40` decision session — these are shared principles every era-to-era transition must follow; they do not supply the specific missing Older Shadows→Vegetative Field or Vegetative Field→New Signal transition texts, which remain open per the paragraph above):

1. **Continuous, never a hard cut.** Lighting/color interpolates across the boundary; only the entry-cave crossing gets the explicit 2–3s eye-adaptation "burn," because only Entry's transition is a literal light-level discontinuity (darkness to daylight) — every other era-to-era transition is a gradual register change, not a light-level jump, and must not borrow the eye-adaptation technique.
2. **Bureaucracy register shifts, never resets.** Each era's own register (Entry "it authorizes" → Birth Yard "it organizes" → Older Shadows "it marks/flags" → Vegetative Field "it anesthetizes" → New Signal "it accuses, then loses control") changes gradually across the boundary zone, never switching instantly at one fixed point.
3. **Density ramps, never jumps.** The outgoing era's density target band (§4) blends toward the incoming era's target band across the transition distance — `VERY_HIGH` Birth Yard does not cut directly to `MEDIUM` Older Shadows at a single node.
4. **Motif handoff is explicit, never implied.** Where a motif or population candidate crosses an era boundary (the empty-bicycle motif, the seven transversal motifs), the outgoing era's own Era Contract must name the handoff; a future Identity Contract may not assume a motif carries forward without that explicit statement.
5. **No transition doubles as an anomaly.** The transition itself is a bureaucratic/atmospheric shift, never the segment's own central anomaly — the one-anomaly-per-segment rule (Track Atlas, generic guardrails) applies at boundaries exactly as it does mid-segment.

## 13. Music/world relationship

**Confirmed, consistent across `DRIFT_3D_REALISM_BIBLE.md`, `DRIFT_3D_LIVING_WORLD_BIBLE.md` §8/§9.2/§12/§18, and `DRIFT_3D_PRODUCT_SPEC.md` — no conflict.**

- One spatial emitter per track, 8–12s crossfade along the spline, one diegetic ambience layer per zone. The diegetic mix lowers as the track's music rises.
- Music stays explicitly triggered by the visitor — approaching a node never starts playback. The explicit audio button remains the one deliberate exception to "no extra UI."
- Cue-sheet-driven reactivity is preferred over generic frequency analysis; frequency analysis alone never decides dramaturgy. Levels 0–3: silent world → active listening → signature cue → sufficient listening.
- The global `AudioPlayerProvider` remains the single protected source of playback truth (Product Spec).

## 14. Anti-patterns (consolidated index)

**Absolute prohibitions at final render** (Realism Bible): visible placeholder cubes/spheres; decorative wireframe; Tron/synthwave aesthetic; arbitrary gradients; emissive color without a diegetic source.

**Final anti-abstract checklist** (Realism Bible): no mesh without a textured PBR material; no emissive light without a diegetic source; every POI silhouette-identifiable at 200m; fog/sky/shadows consistent with the segment's scripted hour; λ only sculpted, never floating/self-luminous; the four zones distinguishable half-squinting by light and palette alone.

**Humor** (§3 above, Living World Bible §4.2): pasted meme, constant textual joke, crudeness-as-sole-device, total cynicism, cultural caricature, punching down, unrelated gag.

**Color** (§6 above): global rainbow, uniform saturation, identical cross-track palette, unsourced neon, arbitrary decorative gradient.

**Biographical** (Living World Bible §2.3): personal data, medical detail, identifiable score-settling, real-person caricature, family exposure, over-explained symbol.

**Reference/influence misuse** (Living World Bible §3.2): imitated film sets, generic sci-fi, recognizable quotes; label pastiche, mascot copies, functionless sticker accumulation; open-air museum, gratuitous references, artwork quotation, random pseudo-graffiti.

**No punishment** (Living World Bible §9.4): situations must not durably block, force a grind, trigger failure, or demand mobile-incompatible precision.

**Camera** (Living World Bible §11): must not remove control, shake constantly, force a long cutscene, hide corridors, or cause motion sickness.

**Global anti-goals** (Living World Bible §14 — the complete list, verbatim): Drift must not become *"un metaverse ; un parc à thèmes ; un musée de références ; un clone de jeu de voiture ; une démo Three.js ; un monde cyberpunk néon ; une succession de dioramas ; un clip qui enlève le contrôle ; un catalogue de shaders ; une carte remplie de texte ; un univers cartoon ; une satire sans tendresse ; une collection de gags ; un monde photoréaliste générique ; un objet techniquement impressionnant mais émotionnellement vide."*

**Product boundary** (Product Spec): *"Track meaning comes from owner-approved artistic contracts, never runtime invention."*

**Surviving gameplay anti-patterns** (§15 below): no ragdoll, no destructible scenery, no particle-based effects, no bounce pads/game-toy mechanics; no lap timers, checkpoints, score, fail state, collectibles, or game UI.

## 15. Surviving gameplay rules (from the two retired visual-doctrine documents)

`DRIFT_3D_ART_DIRECTION.md` and `DRIFT_3D_SET_DESIGN_BLUEPRINT.md` remain `HISTORICAL` for visual doctrine (superseded by the Realism Bible, 2026-07-07) but each document's own header preserves specific gameplay rules. Indexed here verbatim so this document can be the single read for global direction without requiring a trip through 2,560 combined lines of superseded visual content:

- **Driving physics** (Art Direction §14.2, human-approved 2026-07-06): real small-car acceleration/braking, speed-dependent steering, a slip/grip drift model (heading and travel direction separate briefly, then re-align); solid collision against decorative props and the world boundary — the vehicle is stopped/deflected, never just clamped. The vehicle stays a small capsule, never a literal car model.
- **Camera**: fixed oblique orientation; follows by translation, never yaw rotation.
- **No game-toy mechanics**: no ragdoll, no destructible scenery, no particle-based effects, no bounce pads.
- **No game UI**: no lap timers, checkpoints, score, fail state, or collectibles.
- **Proximity stays non-solid**: zones and node markers never block the vehicle; audio only ever starts on explicit user action.
- **Corridors and node protection** (Set Design Blueprint §2/§8): corridors must stay explicit and visible in the plan; vehicle and node visibility come first; props stay out of travel lanes; node centers stay clear; empty space is intentional design, not missing content. Decorative props are solid collision volumes — a prop left inside a lane physically blocks the vehicle, not just clutters the shot.
