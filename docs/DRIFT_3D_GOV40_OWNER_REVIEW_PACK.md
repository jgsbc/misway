## 1. The global narrative arc

A visitor's shadow is scanned at a mineral threshold and declared **"NON CONFORME."** Precisely because of that verdict, they are let through — the world's first rule is that admission comes *via* anomaly, not despite it.

They arrive in **Birth Yard**, a vertical port city that manufactures a role for them and rewards them for occupying it until they stop noticing it wasn't chosen — swarming, dirty, work-pressured, trafficked; a city that "organizes." Inside it, a gym trains, measures and corrects the people inside it, not the reverse — the era's sharpest inversion.

They climb into **Older Shadows** — mountain, altitude, extreme sport, African travel — and for the first time feel alive through risk. But that freedom is quietly measured, compared, insured even as it's felt; the owner's own framing names this precisely: **physical freedom becoming memory** even while it's still happening.

They descend into **Vegetative Field** — identical housing, a chalk quarry, a station with clocks that disagree — where everything works so well that life quietly disappears behind its own maintenance. Its own stated goal: **"the funniest cold and the most unsettling in hindsight."**

They enter **New Signal**, the largest era, in three movements: first **accusation and disorientation** (a forest that assigns blame, ice that stops lying, a well that returns self-confidence, an interchange that saturates into ghost-routes); then **acceptance and persistence** (a storm-light that must be protected, a window that keeps working after the desk is empty, a mirror-maze where the past keeps pace); then **the ocean** — a city that shuts down to one lit window, a coastline where light turns from raw to polished, an edge where a feline shadow keeps the past in view while turning forward, and finally a dawn beach where the whole session's fragments are gathered into an immense λ and let go. **Not a reset — a coastal road reopens toward the world's beginning, changed.**

Threaded through all four eras: two literal cross-era reworks (PANTHERE reworks A WALK IN ZEELAND; RENEE reworks PERDUE — "the point is not to clean away the earlier emotion; it is to let it return in another form"), a recurring empty-bicycle motif, and — as of this correction — a color arc the owner names explicitly: **gold and silver**, and a λ motif that starts exceptional at the threshold and becomes, by New Signal, an ordinary form of the world itself.
