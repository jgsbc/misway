# WORLD_CONTENT.md

**Status:** ACTIVE CONTENT AUTHORITY / CONVERGENCE IN PROGRESS  
**Purpose:** describe what MISWAY contains and what authored content must survive technical migration.

## World structure

MISWAY DRIFT contains an Entry and four canonical eras:

1. **Birth Yard**
2. **Older Shadows**
3. **Vegetative Field**
4. **New Signal**

The accepted catalogue remains 26 tracks plus the Entry ambient segment. Track order, era membership, identities and approved local dramaturgies are preserved from the accepted Era/Track Atlas and Identity Contracts until individually absorbed here.

No migration may silently reorder, rename or reinterpret those territories.

## Geographic capital

The current production world remains runtime truth until replaced by a validated migration.

The `experiment/drift-greybox-fable` branch is an R&D source containing valuable geographic work to recover, especially:

- a large folded peninsula rather than a globally scaled small map;
- a real bay/coast/sea relationship;
- x/z-bounded regions rather than z-only bands;
- mountain, basin and coastal morphology;
- route networks that influence terrain locally;
- terrain depth / seabed logic;
- deterministic spatial generation;
- track-territory and density experiments;
- useful debug probes and immersion findings.

Fable is not a second product and is not to be merged wholesale. Its geography is presumed valuable and should be **EXTRACTED into `/drift` while preserving validated MISWAY content**.

## Creative capital presumed preserved

Unless explicitly re-decided by JG, migration preserves:

- map and topology decisions already accepted;
- era identities and ordering;
- track identities and track-to-era membership;
- approved landmarks and narrative signatures;
- accepted masterframes and visual references;
- EUX GAINENT's accepted identity, cues and living scene;
- relations and transitions between tracks where already accepted;
- validated object/vehicle proportions;
- validated chase camera behavior and driving controls;
- approved audio/cue timing.

A technical defect should first be fixed technically. Geography or narrative must not be redesigned merely to bypass the defect.

## MISWAY content grammar

An era is a world grammar, not a color preset. It may govern:

- geography and morphology;
- architecture;
- vegetation;
- materials;
- population and density;
- road/path language;
- weather and light;
- ambient life and sound;
- anomaly/strangeness rules;
- local behavior.

A track territory should progressively follow:

```text
ERA GRAMMAR
+ TRACK MODIFIERS
+ LANDMARK
+ SIGNATURE
= TRACK TERRITORY
```

A track must not own a separate terrain engine, camera, physics system, audio engine or world runtime.

## Existing accepted proof

**EUX GAINENT** is preserved as the accepted first living track proof slice. Its shared audio clock, cue resolver, signature arbitration and track-local dramaturgy pattern are valuable architectural evidence. Its local visual implementation may later feed reusable primitives, but acceptance is not revoked by extraction.

## Visual production references

The five accepted visual masterframes for Entry, Birth Yard, Older Shadows, Vegetative Field and New Signal remain production references. Shared-kit pilots are technical references only where previously accepted as such; placeholder/low-poly pilot art is not promoted to final foreground art by reuse.
