## Geographic capital

The current production world remains runtime truth until replaced by a validated migration.

The `experiment/drift-greybox-fable` branch is an R&D source containing valuable geographic work to recover, especially:

- a large folded peninsula rather than a globally scaled small map;
- a real bay/coast/sea relationship;
- x/z-bounded regions rather than z-only bands;
- mountain, basin and coastal morphology;
- route networks that influence terrain locally;
- terrain depth / seabed logic;
- deterministic spatial generation;
- track-territory and density experiments;
- useful debug probes and immersion findings.

Fable is not a second product and is not to be merged wholesale. Its geography is presumed valuable and should be **EXTRACTED into the real runtime only when a current spatial lot explicitly needs it**, while preserving validated MISWAY content.

The active spatial convergence authority is now:

- `docs/DRIFT_3D_SPATIAL_BIBLE.md` — human-readable path/placement doctrine and current catalogue reconciliation;
- `docs/DRIFT_3D_SPATIAL_ATLAS.json` — machine-readable era/track positions, placement statuses, route evidence and open spatial decisions.

These documents distinguish current raw topology from effective promoted placement and from owner-accepted spatial decisions. A runtime coordinate alone is not proof of final artistic placement.

## MISWAY content grammar

An era is a world grammar, not a color preset. It may govern:

- geography and morphology;
- architecture;
- vegetation;
- materials;
- population and density;
- road/path language;
- weather and light;
- ambient life and sound;
- anomaly/strangeness rules;
- local behavior.

A track territory should progressively follow:

```text
ERA GRAMMAR
+ TRACK MODIFIERS
+ LANDMARK
+ SIGNATURE
= TRACK TERRITORY
```

A track must not own a separate terrain engine, camera, physics system, audio engine or world runtime.

For spatial work specifically:

```text
ERA GEOGRAPHY
+ ROUTE GRAPH
+ TRACK FOOTPRINT
+ TERRAIN / WATER / COLLIDER CONSTRAINTS
+ AUTHORED APPROACH / EXIT
= DRIVEABLE TRACK TERRITORY
```
