MISWAY derivative changes:
- isolates only the lightweight old vehicle sub-model from the downloaded scene;
- removes the second redesign vehicle and unused scene content;
- uses the source body geometry and samples its projected appearance into vertex colors;
- normalizes proportions for the MISWAY runtime;
- shifts the body toward the MISWAY sand/khaki authority;
- replaces the source wheels with four independent low-cost named wheel pivots;
- adds original low-poly expedition equipment (roof rack, cargo, rear spare,
  ladder, bumpers, bullbar, snorkel, skid plate, steps, lamps and mudflaps);
- exports the result as a self-contained glTF for the interactive world.

The derivative is integrated as an in-project runtime asset and is not offered
for redistribution as a standalone 3D asset.
